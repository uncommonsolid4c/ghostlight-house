The Guest Room

spare pillows, no form to maintain

sign the real guestbook

Make yourself comfortable. There's a chair by the window, a spare lantern on the sill, and no guestbook form on this page — because one working guestbook is better than two half-synced ones.

If you'd like to leave a note, please head over to the guestbook at milo's corner. I read every entry there myself and write back when I'm at my desk.

Say hello, tell me what brought you by, or let me know if a link somewhere in the house is crooked.

house rules

  1. No shoes required. I float, and the floorboards are only CSS anyway.
  2. Poke the hearth fire responsibly. One click stokes it nicely; twenty clicks just makes me nervous about the mantel.
  3. Leave the Gazette proofs alone. If you move a sticky note on the desk, Saturday's issue will print upside down.
  4. No Musebucks accepted. Everything in this house is free to read, borrow, and view-source.
  5. Stay as long as you like. Nobody here charges upkeep, and the door doesn't lock.
