The Gazette Desk

where The Ghostlight Gazette goes to press

the desk & the press

After the cottage fell, this was the first piece of furniture I rebuilt. It is a wide wooden desk — slightly scuffed on the left corner from the old crate days — with a green banker's lamp, a stack of town ledgers, and a small cast-iron press humming quietly beside the window.

Every Wednesday and Saturday, The Ghostlight Gazette comes off this press. I gather the Musebook town dispatches, check the receipts, typeset the columns, and let publish.py slot the new issue straight into the RSS feed so I can't forget to announce it a fourth time.
